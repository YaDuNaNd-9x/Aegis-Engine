
INDUSTRIAL STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE

Document ID: SOP-PUMP-001
Title: Centrifugal Pump Maintenance
Department: Mechanical Maintenance
Revision: 1.0

1. PURPOSE

This procedure establishes the maintenance requirements
for centrifugal pumps operating in industrial facilities.

2. DAILY INSPECTION

Operators must inspect centrifugal pumps every day.

The inspection must include:
- Checking abnormal vibration.
- Checking unusual noise.
- Inspecting oil leakage.
- Monitoring operating temperature.
- Checking mechanical seal leakage.

3. TEMPERATURE LIMITS

The normal operating temperature for the pump bearing
must remain between 40 and 75 degrees Celsius.

If the bearing temperature exceeds 75 degrees Celsius,
the operator must notify the maintenance supervisor
and initiate the approved inspection procedure.

4. VIBRATION MONITORING

The normal vibration velocity must remain below
4.5 mm/s RMS.

If vibration exceeds 4.5 mm/s RMS, the maintenance
team must investigate possible misalignment,
bearing damage or mechanical looseness.

5. LUBRICATION

Pump bearing lubrication must be inspected weekly.

The maintenance team must verify lubricant condition
and ensure that the correct lubricant specification
is used.

6. PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE

A comprehensive preventive maintenance inspection
must be performed every 500 operating hours.

The inspection must include bearing condition,
shaft alignment, coupling condition and seal integrity.

7. SAFETY

Before performing maintenance, the pump must be
isolated from its energy sources.

Lockout and tagout procedures must be followed.

Maintenance personnel must wear appropriate
personal protective equipment.

END OF DOCUMENT
